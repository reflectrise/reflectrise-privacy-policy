Privacy Policy

Welcome to ReflectRise (https://reflectrise.co.uk). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our app and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to operate ReflectRise effectively:

  • Account information: email and display name.
  • Optional profile data: profile picture.
  • User-generated content: journal entries, goals, streaks, settings.
  • Technical data: device type, operating system, usage statistics.

2. Third-Party Services

ReflectRise uses trusted third-party providers including:

  • Firebase Authentication: secure login & account management
  • Firebase Firestore: encrypted cloud data storage
  • Firebase Storage: profile pictures & media uploads
  • Stripe: secure processing of premium subscriptions
  • Analytics tools: anonymous usage insights for improving the app

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the ReflectRise platform
  • To sync journals, goals, themes, and preferences across devices
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To contact you regarding important updates or account notices

4. Data Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase security standards. We implement encryption, secure access controls, and continuous system monitoring.

5. Children’s Privacy

ReflectRise is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected with a new “Effective Date.”
